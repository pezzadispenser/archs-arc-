How it works

  • Every quest cleared earns XP. Pink ones are worth more.
  • Clear every quest in a day for a full clear: +50 XP bonus.
  • Out of the door on time on a college day keeps your on-time combo alive. Weekends don't break it.
  • You get one shield a week. It covers your first missed morning that week, so one bad day doesn't wipe your combo.
  • A finished 25 minute training session is +20 XP. Quest log steps and finished homework score too.
  • XP moves you up the ranks, E to S, then SS.
  • You can tick today and yesterday. After that the day is locked.
